# Track B (CAPRI atomic complexes): status

As of 2026-10-29 the SEPIQ pages say Track B follows standard CAPRI procedures and give no portal, model count, deadline
or file format. We have not registered with CAPRI, so **no Track B submission has been made**.

Material we have: multi-seed Boltz-2 atomic models (3 seeds x 5 samples) for ONU11, ONU13 and ONU23. If a Track B route opens,
we would submit the pose that is most consistent across seeds (most common binding region), not the top-ranked by ipTM,
because ipTM did not reliably rank poses on our proxy complexes.
