## Patterns

- Numbers computed by the hive from the real tables (counts, read totals, depletion) are accurate; the one understatement (ONU13 depletion is roughly 180-420x, not ">20x") errs conservative.
- Drift comes from stale inheritance: the BRIEF "few hundred labels" survived in q3, and "Fab" for 9MTE/9MTX was inferred rather than read from the entity list.
- Benchmark rates are right but easy to mislabel: 31.6% (DockQ>0.23) vs 13.3% (DockQ>=0.80) for AF3 nanobodies; single seed, n=60 (CI roughly 21-45%, worker arithmetic).
- Provenance traps: 0.176 comes from sepiq.uk, not the AntiBinder paper; "portal returns no score" is an absence of statement, not a stated rule; prizes appear on /detail only.
- Worker-run statistics (replicate precision, mimotope oracle, AUROC drops) were not rerun here and stay medium confidence. Protenix seed-scaling numbers are unverified.
- Base rate: 3-5% holds for single-VHH epitopes on modelled residues (5MY6 3.1%, 9MTX 4.3%); the 9MTE two-VHH union is 6.2%.
